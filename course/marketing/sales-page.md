## FAQ

**Do I need to pay for ChatGPT or any other tool?**
No. The lessons work with free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot. Where a feature depends on a paid plan (like some file-upload or data analysis features), the lesson says so and shows a free alternative.

**I've never used AI. Is that okay?**
Yes. Module 0 walks you through setup. The course assumes you've tried it once or twice at most.

**How long does it take?**
About 3 hours of video across short lessons, 3–7 minutes each. Each has a 10-minute exercise, so you can do one lesson at a time.

**Will this help me get a job or better grades?**
The course teaches skills and habits. It can't promise outcomes, since those depend on you, your school and your employers.

**Is using AI for school cheating?**
It depends on your class's rules. The course teaches you how to check your policy and use AI for learning, like explaining concepts, quizzing yourself, and feedback on your own drafts, rather than to submit work that isn't yours.

**Will the lessons go out of date?**
AI tools change fast, so the course teaches habits and prompt structures that work across tools, and includes a Tool Update Guide. Anything specific to a version or plan is flagged.

**Is my data safe when I use AI?**
Not automatically. Module 7 covers what never to paste, how to use placeholders, and how to check each tool's settings.
