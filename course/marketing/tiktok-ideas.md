# 30 TikTok Video Ideas

Each has a 3-second hook, the demo to show and a call to action (CTA). Use fictional data in every demo. Don't make income or grade promises. Keep "CTA" honest, like "Link in bio for the free prompt" or "Follow for more."

| # | Hook (first 3 seconds) | Demo to show | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "This is why your AI emails sound fake." | Show a generic AI email, then a version using your voice note. Highlight the changed phrases. | "Follow for the voice-note trick." |
| 2 | "Stop typing 'write me an email.'" | Weak prompt vs. 4-part prompt, side by side. | "Comment 'formula' and I'll send the template." |
| 3 | "The 4 words that fix any prompt." | Show Role, Context, Task, Format appearing on screen with a live example. | "Save this for later." |
| 4 | "Turn 3 bullets into an email in 20 seconds." | Screen-record bullets to finished email, then edit one line. | "Try it and tell me how long it took." |
| 5 | "AI wrote this Excel formula in 10 seconds." | Describe a goal in plain English; paste the formula; drag it down. | "Follow for more spreadsheet shortcuts." |
| 6 | "Never trust AI math. Do this instead." | Ask AI for a total, then compare with `=SUM`. | "Save this before your next spreadsheet." |
| 7 | "This spreadsheet is a mess. Watch." | Clean names with `TRIM` and `PROPER` from an AI-provided formula. | "Want the practice sheet? Link in bio." |
| 8 | "That Excel error isn't as scary as it looks." | Paste an error and formula, show the explanation and fix. | "Comment your worst Excel error." |
| 9 | "Your resume bullets are boring. Fix them without lying." | Show raw notes to strong bullets, with the "don't invent numbers" rule. | "Follow for honest resume tips." |
| 10 | "Don't let AI invent your resume." | Show an AI adding a fake percentage, then the truth-check prompt catching it. | "Save this." |
| 11 | "Tailor your resume in 5 minutes." | Paste a posting, extract requirements, show the gap analysis. | "Link in bio for the match checklist." |
| 12 | "Cover letters that don't sound like AI." | Show the 3-ingredient notes method, then the finished draft. | "Comment 'cover letter' for the prompt." |
| 13 | "Fix your LinkedIn headline." | Default headline to five options to pick one. | "Follow for more LinkedIn tips." |
| 14 | "Free mock interview at 11 p.m." | Run one question and feedback using STAR. | "Try it tonight." |
| 15 | "Make flashcards from your notes in 60 seconds." | Paste notes, get a table, copy it to a flashcard app. | "Save for finals week." |
| 16 | "Let AI quiz you the night before the test." | Show a quiz one question at a time, with a wrong answer and feedback. | "Follow for study tips." |
| 17 | "Feeling behind? Make a study plan." | Enter deadlines and hours to a table plan, then paste it in a calendar. | "Send this to a friend who's stressed." |
| 18 | "The 'teach it back' trick." | Read an explanation, then explain it yourself, then get feedback. | "Try it with one topic today." |
| 19 | "How to use AI for school without cheating." | Show the three-question check (allowed? my thinking? can I explain it?). | "Follow for honest AI tips." |
| 20 | "AI told me a fake source. Here's how I caught it." | Ask for sources and search for each one. Show one that doesn't exist. | "Save the 3-step fact-check." |
| 21 | "Confident doesn't mean correct." | Talk over a slide with the three-step Flag, Verify, Decide method. | "Follow for more." |
| 22 | "Never paste these 5 things into AI." | List the five: passwords, IDs and account numbers, medical info, others' private details, confidential work info. | "Send to someone who uses ChatGPT." |
| 23 | "I compared AI forecasts to what really happened." | (Only if your firm allows this) Talk over a general, non-confidential slide: confident predictions can still be off. Say "in my testing." | "Follow for honest AI takes." |
| 24 | "Side project idea? Try this first." | Run the skeptical-friend prompt on an idea. Show the risks list. | "Comment your idea." |
| 25 | "A 30-day test plan for any side hustle." | Generate a plan cut down to 5 hours a week. | "Save this." |
| 26 | "Write product descriptions without fake claims." | Show the "use only these facts" rule, and a robotic vs. real version. | "Follow for small business tips." |
| 27 | "Reply to customers in half the time." | Turn policies into 3 reply templates. | "Save for later." |
| 28 | "Your first 10-minute AI win." | Take a real small task, prompt, edit, done. Timer on screen. | "Try one and comment what you did." |
| 29 | "Three AI mistakes beginners make." | (1) Vague prompts, (2) Trusting facts, (3) Pasting private info. Quick demo of each. | "Follow for the fixes." |
| 30 | "Build your own prompt library." | Show a notes app with titles, when to use, prompt with brackets. | "Link in bio for the free prompt cheat sheet" (or your lead magnet). |
