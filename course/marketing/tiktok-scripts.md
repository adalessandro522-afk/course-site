# 10 Fully Scripted TikToks (under 45 seconds each)

Each script is about 50–75 words spoken, so it runs roughly 25–35 seconds at a natural pace (you can add a beat or a longer demo shot and stay under 45). Use fake data only. **[SCREEN]** = what to record. Add captions to every video.

---

### 1. Your AI emails sound fake. Here's why.
[SCREEN: On camera, then a generic AI email.]

Your AI emails sound fake because the AI has never read anything you've written. [SCREEN: Highlight "I hope this email finds you well."] So it defaults to this. Here's the fix. [SCREEN: Paste three of your own messages.] Paste in a few things you've written and say, "Describe my writing style in five bullets." [SCREEN: Show the bullets.] Save that. Next time, say, "Write this email in this style, no clichés." [SCREEN: Show the improved email.] Then read it out loud, and change anything you wouldn't say. Follow for more no-hype AI tips.

### 2. The prompt formula
[SCREEN: On camera.]

Stop typing "write me an email." Use these four words instead. [SCREEN: Text on screen: Role.] Role: who should the AI act like? [Context.] Context: what's the situation? [Task.] Task: what exactly do you want? [Format.] Format: how long, what style? Watch. [SCREEN: Run the interview example.] "Act as an interviewer for a marketing job. I'm a college senior with one internship. Ask me five questions, one at a time." Way better than "help me prepare." Save this video and try it today.

### 3. Never trust AI math
[SCREEN: Spreadsheet with numbers, chat window.]

I asked AI to add up this column. [SCREEN: Show its answer.] It sounded sure. Let's check. [SCREEN: Type =SUM. Show a different number, or matching.] Here's my rule: AI suggests, the spreadsheet calculates. [SCREEN: Ask for the formula instead.] Ask it for the formula, not the answer. Paste the formula, and now you can see the calculation and check it. Then test two rows by hand. Never trust a number you can't check. Follow for more spreadsheet tips.

### 4. Resume bullets without lying
[SCREEN: On camera, then "Worked front counter" as a weak bullet.]

Your resume bullet says "worked front counter." Boring. But do not ask AI to make it sound impressive, because it'll invent numbers. [SCREEN: Show a fake "increased sales 40%".] Instead, write what you actually did. Trained two new hires. Suggested a menu sign the manager used. [SCREEN: Paste notes and the prompt.] Then tell AI: use only these facts, no new numbers. [SCREEN: Show real bullets.] Now every line is true, and you can explain it in an interview. Follow for honest career tips.

### 5. Flashcards in 60 seconds
[SCREEN: Notes page.]

Finals week. Here are my notes. [SCREEN: Paste notes into chat.] Prompt: "Using only these notes, make fifteen flashcards in a two-column table." [SCREEN: Show the table.] Copy it into a spreadsheet, or import it into a flashcard app. [SCREEN: Copy and paste.] Then, "Quiz me one question at a time." [SCREEN: Answer, get feedback.] One warning: check the cards. AI can make mistakes, so compare them to your notes. Save this for finals.

### 6. AI made up a source
[SCREEN: On camera, chat.]

I asked AI for three sources on sleep and grades. [SCREEN: Show them.] They look perfect. Let's search. [SCREEN: Search the first: found.] Real. [SCREEN: Search the second: nothing.] Nothing. [SCREEN: Third: real article but different numbers.] Real article, wrong statistic. That's a hallucination: confident and false. So: flag every claim, verify with a real source, and cut what you can't confirm. Don't ask the AI "are you sure?" Follow for more.

### 7. Never paste these 5 things
[SCREEN: On camera with a numbered list.]

Never paste these five things into AI. One: passwords or login codes. Two: ID or account numbers. Three: medical information. Four: other people's private details. Five: confidential work or school stuff. [SCREEN: Show a text with [CLIENT] and [MANAGER].] Use placeholders instead of real names. And check your tool's privacy settings today. [SCREEN: Open settings.] It takes ten seconds and it's hard to undo later. Send this to a friend who uses ChatGPT.

### 8. Study plan when you're behind

Three exams. Two deadlines. Zero plan. Do this. [SCREEN: Type deadlines, work hours, weak topics.] Tell AI your deadlines, when you work, and how many hours you can really study. Ask for a realistic plan with breaks. [SCREEN: Show the table.] Then paste it into your calendar. [SCREEN: Paste.] A plan in a chat you'll never open doesn't count. Missed a day? Tell it and re-plan. Send this to someone who's stressed.

### 9. Test a side hustle idea
[SCREEN: On camera.]

Got a side hustle idea? Don't ask AI if it's good, because it'll just say yes. [SCREEN: Type the skeptical-friend prompt.] Ask: "Act as a skeptical friend. Give me five reasons this might not work." [SCREEN: Show the answer.] Now you have real questions. Who's the customer? Who else does it? Turn them into a to-do list, and talk to five real people this week. AI can't tell you if people will buy. Only people can. Comment your idea.

### 10. Your first 10-minute win
[SCREEN: Timer on screen, ten minutes.]

Let's do an AI win in ten minutes. Pick one small task. An email, a summary, a plan. [SCREEN: Type a 4-part prompt.] Give it who you are, the situation, what you want and the format. [SCREEN: Show the result.] Now edit it. Change two things so it sounds like you, and check every fact. [SCREEN: Timer stops.] Done. That's the whole method: give it real info, then review. Try it and comment what you did.
