# Pricing Tiers and Upsell Suggestions

**Recommendation:** launch with one main product at **$49** and one simple optional upsell. Keep it simple until you have real student feedback. Don't imply income outcomes, and only advertise features you will deliver.

## Core options

| Tier | Price | What's included | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free lead magnet** | $0 | Prompting Cheat Sheet + 5 sample prompts | For TikTok bio link and email sign-ups |
| **Starter (optional)** | $19–$29 | Prompt Library + templates and checklists only (no video) | Low-risk entry for people not ready for the full course. Skip it if it cannibalizes the $49 course |
| **Main course** | **$49** | All 35 video lessons + all downloads | Your flagship |
| **Course + Review Pack (upsell)** | $79–$99 | Main course + extras below | Only offer if you can deliver personally |

## Upsell ideas (pick 1–2)

1. **Resume and LinkedIn review** ($): a personal 15-minute review or written feedback on a student's resume. Sets clear limits, since you can't guarantee interviews or jobs.
2. **Extended prompt pack:** 100+ extra prompts, for example a finance-student pack (reading filings, summarizing earnings materials, building study guides, checking assumptions, always with verification steps) since it fits your background. Don't present anything as investment advice.
3. **Spreadsheet Starter Kit:** ready-made templates (budget, tracker, grade calculator) plus a short video walkthrough.
4. **Live Q&A session:** one recorded group session each quarter for updates as tools change.

## Practical notes

- **Order bump at checkout ($7–$12):** a printable "AI Fact-Check and Privacy Checklist" bundle, or the Spreadsheet Starter Kit.
- **Launch pricing:** if you discount for launch week, make it real and end it on the stated date.
- **Refund policy:** decide one clearly and state it on the sales page. Check your platform's rules.
- **Testimonials:** collect from real students after they finish, with written permission. Don't use invented ones.
- **Taxes and platform fees:** check what your platform handles (sales tax, payment fees) and get proper advice as your sales grow.
- **Review after 30–60 days:** look at completion rates, questions and refund reasons, then adjust the price and content based on real data.
