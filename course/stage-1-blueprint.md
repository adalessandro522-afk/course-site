## 4. Module and lesson outline

Length is the estimated video time per lesson.

### Module 0: Start Here (12 min)
| # | Lesson | Objective | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | Welcome and how to use this course | Know what you'll be able to do, how the lessons are laid out, and the honesty rules (accuracy, privacy, integrity) | 3 min |
| 0.2 | Set up your AI toolkit | Create a free account on at least one assistant and find the chat box, file upload and new-chat controls | 5 min |
| 0.3 | Your first 10-minute win | Turn one real task from your week into a usable result with a single prompt | 4 min |

### Module 1: Prompting Foundations (24 min) — *Outcome 1*
| # | Lesson | Objective | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | What AI is actually doing | Explain in plain English why AI sounds confident and can still be wrong | 4 min |
| 1.2 | The 4-part prompt formula | Write prompts with Role, Context, Task and Format | 6 min |
| 1.3 | Examples, tone and not sounding robotic | Use examples and a personal style note to get natural writing | 6 min |
| 1.4 | Fixing bad outputs | Use follow-up prompts to shorten, correct, redo or redirect a weak answer | 5 min |
| 1.5 | Build your personal prompt library | Save your best prompts in a reusable, organized place | 3 min |

### Module 2: Emails in Minutes (21 min) — *Outcome 2*
| # | Lesson | Objective | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | From bullet points to a finished email | Draft a professional email from rough notes | 5 min |
| 2.2 | Matching your voice and the situation | Adjust tone (formal, warm, firm) and match your own writing style | 5 min |
| 2.3 | Replies, long threads and tricky emails | Summarize a thread and draft replies for follow-ups, declines and awkward requests | 6 min |
| 2.4 | Polish without over-editing | Proofread and tighten an email while keeping your meaning, and know when to send it as is | 5 min |

### Module 3: Spreadsheets Made Simple (27 min) — *Outcome 3*
| # | Lesson | Objective | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Get formulas written and explained | Describe a goal in words and get a working Excel or Google Sheets formula, with an explanation | 6 min |
| 3.2 | Clean messy data | Fix inconsistent names, dates and duplicates, using formulas or a safe copy | 6 min |
| 3.3 | Analyze and summarize your data | Get trends, summaries and chart ideas, and know what to verify | 6 min |
| 3.4 | Build a simple tracker or budget | Set up a starter spreadsheet layout with AI help | 5 min |
| 3.5 | Debug errors and check the math | Fix formula errors and spot-check AI's numbers | 4 min |

### Module 4: Resume, Cover Letter and LinkedIn (25 min) — *Outcome 4*
| # | Lesson | Objective | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Turn real experience into strong resume bullets | Write specific, honest bullets from your actual work, school and projects | 7 min |
| 4.2 | Tailor your resume to a job posting | Match keywords and priorities without inventing experience | 5 min |
| 4.3 | Cover letters that don't sound generic | Draft a short, specific cover letter and edit it into your voice | 6 min |
| 4.4 | Your LinkedIn profile | Write a headline, About section and experience entries | 4 min |
| 4.5 | Interview practice partner | Run mock interview questions and get feedback on your answers | 3 min |

### Module 5: Study Smarter (21 min) — *Outcome 5*
| # | Lesson | Objective | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Summarize and explain hard material | Turn dense notes or readings into clear explanations at your level | 6 min |
| 5.2 | Flashcards and practice quizzes | Generate self-tests from your own notes and check them for errors | 6 min |
| 5.3 | Build a realistic study plan | Turn an exam date and your available hours into a weekly plan | 5 min |
| 5.4 | Learn with AI, not instead of learning | Know your school's rules and use AI in ways that build understanding | 4 min |

### Module 6: Side Projects and Small Business (22 min) — *Outcome 6*
| # | Lesson | Objective | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Brainstorm and pressure-test an idea | Generate ideas, then have AI challenge yours and list what you'd need to check | 6 min |
| 6.2 | Turn the idea into a 30-day plan | Get a simple weekly plan with clear tasks and a first small test | 5 min |
| 6.3 | Marketing copy and content | Draft a product description, bio and post ideas in your own voice | 6 min |
| 6.4 | Customer messages and simple operations | Make reusable templates for inquiries, FAQs and order follow-ups | 5 min |

### Module 7: AI's Limits: Use It Wisely (16 min) — *Outcome 7*
| # | Lesson | Objective | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | Hallucinations and the 3-step fact-check | Spot made-up facts and sources, and verify anything that matters | 6 min |
| 7.2 | Privacy: what never to paste | Decide what information to remove or avoid, and check your tool's privacy settings | 5 min |
| 7.3 | Integrity and when not to use AI | Follow school and workplace rules, disclose when required, and know when to skip AI | 5 min |

### Module 8: Put It All Together (7 min)
| # | Lesson | Objective | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8.1 | Capstone: your personal AI workflow | Choose your 5 most useful prompts and build a weekly routine | 5 min |
| 8.2 | Next steps | Know how to keep improving and how to adapt as tools change | 2 min |

**Total: 35 lessons, about 2 h 55 min.**

Every learning outcome maps to a module, and accuracy and privacy reminders also appear in the earlier modules, not only in Module 7.
