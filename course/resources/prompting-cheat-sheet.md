# Prompting Cheat Sheet (one page)

## The 4-part prompt: R · C · T · F

| Part | Ask yourself | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Role** | Who should the AI act like? | "Act as a patient tutor." |
| **Context** | What does it need to know? | "I'm a college junior. Exam is Friday." |
| **Task** | What's the one action? | "Quiz me on these notes." |
| **Format** | What should the result look like? | "One question at a time, under 40 words." |

**Not sure what to include?** Add: *"Before you answer, ask me any questions you need to do this well."*

## Fix-it toolbox

- **Shorter:** "Cut to [X] words. Keep [key points]."
- **Tone:** "Make it [warmer / more direct / more formal]."
- **Point at it:** "The sentence '…' sounds pushy. Rewrite only that."
- **Add:** "You left out [fact]. Add it."
- **Options:** "Give me 3 versions labeled A, B, C."
- **Check:** "Check your work and list anything you're unsure about."
- **Reset:** Long or confused chat? Start a new chat and paste the best version.

## Not sounding robotic

1. Paste 2–3 samples of your own writing and ask for a style description.
2. Save it as "My voice."
3. Say what to avoid: clichés, buzzwords, "I hope this email finds you well."
4. **Read it out loud.** Change anything you wouldn't say.

## Check it. Protect it. Own it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Check it** | Flag numbers, names, dates, quotes, sources. Verify each against a reliable source (not the AI). Can't verify? Cut it. |
| **Protect it** | Never paste passwords, ID or account numbers, medical info, other people's private details, or confidential work or school material. Use [PLACEHOLDERS]. |
| **Own it** | Follow your class and workplace rules. Make sure the thinking is yours. Could you explain it without AI open? |

## Good fit vs. be careful vs. skip

- **Good fit:** first drafts, brainstorming, rewriting, summarizing your own material, explaining, practice questions.
- **Be careful:** facts, numbers, sources, private info, rules-bound work.
- **Skip:** high-stakes medical, legal or financial decisions on your own; work that must be yours alone; personal messages where sincerity is the point.

## Spreadsheet rule

**AI suggests. The spreadsheet calculates.** Ask for formulas, not answers, then test on 2–3 rows by hand.

## Universal add-on

> "Don't invent facts. Use [PLACEHOLDER] where you need information from me."

*Interfaces and features change and some depend on paid plans. The habits above still work.*
