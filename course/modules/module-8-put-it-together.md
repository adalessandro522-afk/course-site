# Module 8: Put It All Together (7 min)

---

## Lesson 8.1: Capstone: your personal AI workflow (5 min)

### Script

[SCREEN: You on camera.]

You made it. Let's turn everything into a routine you can actually use, because the goal isn't to remember 30 lessons. It's to have a small system.

[SCREEN: Open your prompt library document. Show the categories.]

Step one: choose your top five. Look at your prompt library and pick the five prompts you'd use most. Maybe an email draft, a follow-up, a study quiz, a resume bullet builder, and a fact-check prompt. Star them.

[SCREEN: Show a weekly routine table.]

Step two: attach them to your week. Monday morning, plan the week. Before class or work, summarize what you need to know. When an email is stressing you out, use your email prompt. Sunday evening, review and update your prompts. A habit beats a list.

[SCREEN: Show the "Check it. Protect it. Own it." slide.]

Step three: keep the three rules. Check it. Protect it. Own it. Put them at the top of your prompt library where you'll see them.

[SCREEN: Show a one-page "My AI Rules" doc.]

Step four: write your own rules. Three to five lines. For example: "I don't paste private information. I verify numbers and sources. I follow each class's AI policy. I always edit AI writing so it sounds like me. I don't use AI for personal apologies."

[SCREEN: Show a 30-day challenge.]

Step five: the 30-day challenge. Use AI for one real task a day for two weeks, and each time note what worked, what didn't and what you changed. You'll learn faster than by watching more videos.

[SCREEN: On camera.]

Do the capstone task now. Ten minutes. Pick your top five, write your rules, and schedule one time this week to use AI on something real.

### Worked example
**Maya's top five:** (1) email from bullets, (2) polite follow-up, (3) study quiz from notes, (4) resume bullet builder with truth check, (5) list every claim to verify.

**Maya's rules:** No private info. Verify numbers. Follow class policies. Edit until it sounds like me. Ask if unsure.

### Prompt template
```
Based on how I spend my week: [classes/work/side project/job search], suggest a realistic routine for using AI on 4–5 recurring tasks, no more than 15 minutes a day. For each, name the type of prompt I'd use and one accuracy or privacy check.
```

### Do it in 10 minutes
Pick your top five prompts, write your personal rules and schedule your first use.

### Quiz
1. **What are the three rules?** Check it, protect it, own it.
2. **Why choose only five prompts?** A small set you actually use beats a long list you ignore.
3. **What should you review weekly?** Your prompts. Update what worked and fix what didn't.

## Lesson 8.2: Next steps (2 min)

### Script

[SCREEN: You on camera.]

That's the course. Quick recap. You can write clear prompts and fix weak answers. You can draft emails faster, work with spreadsheets, build career documents that reflect your real experience, study more actively, plan a side project, and check AI's work.

Here's how to keep improving. Use it on real tasks, and keep your prompt library up to date. Tools change constantly, so when your app looks different, look for the same basics: the message box, uploads, new chat, settings. The habits in this course still apply. And stay skeptical. As AI gets better, checking gets more important, not less.

[SCREEN: Show the Tool Update Guide and the community/support link.]

There's a Tool Update Guide in your downloads to help when things change. If you have questions, reach me at [your support email or handle].

One more thing. If this helped you, tell a friend, or send me a message about what you tried. I read them. And I'd appreciate an honest review, including what could be better.

Thanks for learning with me. Go do something useful with your next ten minutes.

### Do it in 10 minutes
Send one email or message you drafted with your workflow, or write down one thing you'll try tomorrow.

### Quiz
1. **What should you do when an app's interface changes?** Look for the same basics and consult the Tool Update Guide.
2. **Does better AI reduce the need to check its work?** No. Checking stays important.
3. **What's one habit that keeps your skills growing?** Use AI on real tasks and keep updating your prompt library.
